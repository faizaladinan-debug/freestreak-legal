Delete Your Account and Data

FreeStreak lets you permanently delete all of your data directly inside the app, at any time.

To delete everything

Open FreeStreak, go to Settings → Delete All Data, and confirm. This permanently erases every relapse, urge, check-in, habit, and habit-completion record, and resets your streak and personal notes. This cannot be undone.

To clear just your streak

Open FreeStreak, go to Settings → Reset Streak, which clears your streak and relapse history while keeping badges, journal, and habits.

Need help?

If you have any trouble deleting your data in the app, email us at freestreak4u@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.
